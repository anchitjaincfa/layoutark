# Contributing

Keep the product local-first and evidence-led.

- Do not add uploads, accounts, analytics, beacons, or document conversion without a separately reviewed architecture change.
- Never add real customer manifests or documents.
- Changes to `schema/manifest.v1.schema.json` require a new schema version.
- User-visible product claims must be recorded in `content/claims.json`.
- Planned features must remain visibly planned.
